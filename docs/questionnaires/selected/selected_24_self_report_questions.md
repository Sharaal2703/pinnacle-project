# MindBridge Self-Report Questions (24 items, Classes 6–8)

**Structure:** 3 items for each of the 8 intelligence types (8 × 3 = 24).

**Response scale (use this one scale for every item):**

| Rating | Label |
|---|---|
| 1 | Strongly disagree |
| 2 | Disagree |
| 3 | Neutral |
| 4 | Agree |
| 5 | Strongly agree |

Scoring follows design decision D3: response weight = (rating − 1) / 4.

**Note:** These are preference statements, not tests of ability. There are no right or wrong answers. The items are taken word for word from the three source questionnaires listed in the table. They have not been validated for MindBridge and should be reviewed by a teacher and pilot-tested before use.

## Selection notes

- **Age-appropriate wording:** each item is a single, simple statement in everyday language. I chose items about school, hobbies and friends, and left out content that is too mature or personal for 11–14 year olds.
- **One idea per item:** I skipped statements that ask about two things at once, such as "I enjoy singing and I sing well". A student could agree with one half and disagree with the other.
- **Three different sides of each intelligence:** for example, Musical covers noticing sounds, picking up melodies, and learning through song. Bodily-Kinesthetic covers hands-on learning, sport and dance, and working with the hands.
- **Items tied to learning:** where possible I chose items about how a student learns, since the profile will recommend learning content.
- **Presentation:** do not show the items grouped by intelligence. The question selector should mix their order, and the intelligence labels should stay on the server only.
